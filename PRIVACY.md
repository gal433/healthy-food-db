Healthy privacy policy Last updated: 2 November 2026
Healthy is a food and workout log made by Gal, an individual developer in Portugal. Contact: gal@yaco.bi.
What stays on your phone. Everything you enter (foods, meals, weight, goals, workouts) is stored only on your phone. There is no account, and the developer has no server that receives your data. Backups are files you create and keep yourself.
What leaves your phone. • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode or search for a food that isn't saved on your phone, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. Nothing else is sent. Their privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy • Food database downloads: when you download the offline food database, your phone downloads files from GitHub (github.com). No personal data is sent. • Diagnostic log: the app keeps a log on your phone. It is only shared if you tap Copy or Share and send it yourself.
Camera. The camera is used only to scan barcodes and nutrition labels, and images are processed on the phone and not stored or sent anywhere.
No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Healthy contains no advertising or analytics code and does not sell or share your data.
Children. Healthy is not directed at children under 16.
Deleting your data. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings. Because nothing is stored by the developer, there is nothing else to delete.
Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
